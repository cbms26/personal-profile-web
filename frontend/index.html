About Me

Hello! I'm Ngawang Tenzin (CBMS), a tech-savy guy with a pretty unique journey from the mountains of Bhutan to the tech scene in Australia. My path from Electronics Engineering to mastering IT at James Cook University has shaped how I approach problem-solving and come up with creative and practical solutions.

My Journey

Ever since I was about 10 I loved watching my dad type on his old IBM computer. I was mesmerized by the little scribbling animation at the bottom of the Word document that moved whenever he typed and stopped when he paused. It was such a small thing but to me it was pure magic. After finishing my Master's I realized that early fascination was actually a spark of curiosity about how things work which quietly shaped my passion for technology.

My Appraoch

I'm all about thoughtful coding which means not just figuring out how stuff works but really understanding why it works that way. Since I come from an electronics background I get both the logic behind systems and the creative side of user experience. Whether I'm tackling a tricky algorithm or designing a user interface I always ask myself how people will actually use this.
